Privacy Policy — Toppled!

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Toppled! is a casual physics stacking game. This page explains what data the game does and does not collect.

What we collect

Toppled! does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game does not require an account, login, or any personal details to play.

Local save data

Your best score, coin total, and upgrade progress are saved using your browser or device's local storage. This data stays on your device only — it is never sent to us or any server, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data will erase it.

Third-party platforms

Toppled! may be distributed through third-party platforms (such as Google Play, CrazyGames, or Poki). Those platforms may collect standard device, usage, or advertising data under their own privacy policies, separate from this one. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of whichever platform you downloaded or played the game from.

Advertising

If a future version of Toppled! includes ads, they will be served through a third-party ad network's SDK, which may collect data (such as advertising identifiers) as described in that network's own privacy policy. This version does not currently display ads.

Children's privacy

Toppled! is a general-audience casual game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
